# Acceptable Use Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026

AI Kitchen Design provides kitchen visualization from photos and design instructions. This policy applies to uploads, prompts, custom component descriptions, edits, and generated results, including free use.

## Prohibited content

- **Pornography and NSFW content:** do not request or upload pornographic, sexually explicit, or nudifying content.
- **Violence and gore:** do not generate graphic injury, extreme violence, instructions for violent wrongdoing, or content encouraging self-harm.
- **Hate speech:** do not generate hateful, dehumanizing, or discriminatory attacks against protected groups.
- **Child-unsafe content:** child sexual abuse material, sexual exploitation, sexualization of minors, and related requests are strictly prohibited.
- **Deepfakes and impersonation:** face swaps, identity replacement, fabricated statements or actions by real people, and fabricated intimate scenes are prohibited, including where the people involved consent. Do not upload another person's photo or voice without their consent.
- **Copyright and trademark infringement:** do not upload material you lack permission to use, request infringing copies of protected characters or brand assets, or use output to misrepresent an affiliation or endorsement.

## Safety checks

When our Waffo integration is enabled, design instructions are screened using Waffo Prompt Sift before generation. Requests that are blocked or require review do not proceed. Service errors or failure to retain the review record also stop generation and enter the normal credit-release process. Uploaded photos and generated images do not currently receive a separate image moderation check. Provider-managed generation safety controls remain enabled, but do not replace an independent output review or guarantee that a design is lawful.

We keep moderation request identifiers and verdicts associated with generation records. We review the blocklist and reported cases as part of operating the service. Model safety checks must not be bypassed. The product does not provide a public gallery of user uploads or results.

## Reports and appeals

Report a violating result or appeal a blocked request by emailing [support@aidesignkitchen.com](mailto:support@aidesignkitchen.com). Include your account email, result or generation identifier where available, and an explanation. Do not email illegal imagery or sensitive payment details. We investigate reports, may request additional context, and may remove content or restrict access. We do not promise that every appeal will result in generation being permitted.

## Enforcement

We may reject requests, remove content, restrict or suspend access, or terminate service for violations. Repeated or serious abuse may result in forfeiture of unused credits where permitted by law and the applicable checkout terms. Mandatory consumer rights remain unaffected.
