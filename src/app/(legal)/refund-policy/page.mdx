# Refund and Cancellation Policy

This policy applies to design credits purchased for AI Kitchen Design at aidesignkitchen.com.

## One-time purchases

Credit packs are purchased once and do not renew automatically. You can close checkout before completing payment. After payment is confirmed, credits are added to the account used to place the order and expire one month after payment.

## When to contact us

- **Missing credits.** If you paid and your credits did not arrive, contact us so we can check the order and correct the delivery. If we cannot deliver the credits, we can help you request a refund.
- **Duplicate or incorrect charges.** If you believe you were charged twice for the same purchase or charged the wrong amount, send the relevant order details so we can verify the charges and help resolve the issue.
- **Change of mind, used credits, or expired credits.** These situations do not automatically qualify for a refund. We review requests individually, taking into account how many credits have been used, whether there was a payment or delivery issue, the applicable checkout terms, and your rights under applicable law.

## How to request a refund

Email `support@aidesignkitchen.com` with your account email, order number or receipt reference, purchase date, and a brief explanation. For suspected duplicate charges, include both receipt references if available. Do not send your full card number, card security code, or password.

For Waffo Pancake purchases, you can also contact [Waffo support](mailto:support@waffo.ai). For older purchases through Dodo Payments, use [Dodo Payments support](https://dodopayments.com/support/why-has-dodo-payments-charged-me). If a refund is approved, the original payment provider processes it to the original payment method. Refunded purchases may have the corresponding unused credits removed; used credits and exceptional cases are reviewed individually.

Nothing in this policy limits any mandatory consumer rights, including any right to cancel or receive a refund under applicable law.
