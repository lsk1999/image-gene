# Privacy Policy for AI Kitchen Design

Effective date: November 14, 2026

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how AI Kitchen Design collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the website and its AI kitchen visualization service.

## Information We Collect

### Account information

We may collect your name, email address, profile image, account identifier, and authentication information when you create or use an account.

### Kitchen design content

The service processes kitchen photos you upload, design settings, written preferences, edit instructions, generated images, and related generation records. This content is used to provide the requested kitchen design, save results to your account, and support later refinements.

Only upload images that you have the right to use. Avoid including people, confidential documents, addresses, or other unnecessary personal information in kitchen photos.

### Usage and technical information

We may collect information about how you use the service, including features used, generation status, timestamps, browser type, device information, IP address, cookies, and diagnostic data. We use this information to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service.

### Payment information

If you purchase a plan or credits, payment providers process payment details on our behalf. We receive transaction information such as the product purchased, payment status, amount, currency, and billing-related identifiers. We do not directly store complete payment card details.

## How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

- provide AI kitchen generation, editing, storage, and account history;
- authenticate users and protect accounts;
- process purchases and maintain transaction records;
- monitor reliability, prevent abuse, and enforce service limits;
- respond to support requests; and
- comply with legal obligations.

## Service Providers

We use third-party providers to operate the service. Kitchen photos, prompts, and generated content may be processed by AI inference providers. Files may be stored by cloud storage providers. Authentication, database, hosting, analytics, and payment providers may process the information needed for their services.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices. We share only the information reasonably needed to provide the relevant service.

## Storage and Retention

Kitchen photos, generated results, account records, and generation settings may be stored so that you can access saved designs. We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security and transaction records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations.

## Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information. No transmission or storage method can guarantee absolute security.

## Your Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information. You may also manage browser cookies through your browser settings. Some information may be retained when required for security, transaction records, dispute resolution, or legal compliance.

## Children

The service is not directed to children under the age required to consent to online services in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the service changes. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the latest published version.
